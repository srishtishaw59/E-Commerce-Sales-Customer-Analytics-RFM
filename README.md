# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics (RFM Analysis)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales performance and customer purchasing behavior using **SQL and Power BI**. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights, identify valuable customers, and understand customer purchasing patterns through **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis**.

By combining data analysis with interactive visualization, this project helps businesses make data-driven decisions related to customer segmentation, retention, sales performance, and marketing strategy.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-value and loyal customers.
- Segment customers using RFM Analysis.
- Analyze customer recency, frequency, and monetary value.
- Identify opportunities for customer retention and targeted marketing.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **SQL** – Data extraction, transformation, aggregation, and analysis.
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX** – KPI calculations, measures, and analytical metrics.
- **RFM Analysis** – Customer segmentation based on purchasing behavior.

## 📊 RFM Analysis

RFM stands for:

- **Recency (R):** How recently a customer made a purchase.
- **Frequency (F):** How frequently a customer purchases.
- **Monetary (M):** How much money a customer spends.

RFM analysis helps segment customers based on their purchasing behavior and identify different customer groups for targeted business strategies.
